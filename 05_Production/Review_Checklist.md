# Production review checklist

## Before detailed animation

- [ ] The hypothetical rule, onset, duration, and scope are stated.
- [ ] Assumptions distinguish fictional changes from factual quantities.
- [ ] The first shot shows the subject and the premise is readable.
- [ ] The planned sequence offers distinct consequences.
- [ ] The ending connects to the premise or an established object.
- [ ] Each shot adds information or visibly progresses.
- [ ] Cumulative screen time and simulation time agree with the plan.
- [ ] Slow motion and time skips are labeled when needed.
- [ ] Greg's location, ordinary goal, consequence, reaction, and role are clear.
- [ ] Greg's final design status is represented accurately.
- [ ] Required assets and difficult effects are identified.

## Before final export

- [ ] Camera geography, scale, motion, and damage continuity are coherent.
- [ ] Characters react to events they could notice.
- [ ] UI uses readable labels, values, units, and contrast.
- [ ] Readouts track the event and milestone words occur at the correct time.
- [ ] Captions remain visible long enough to read.
- [ ] Text, numbers, spelling, and factual claims have been checked.
- [ ] Dialogue is intelligible and sound arrivals match action.
- [ ] Mix perspective changes with the camera when appropriate.
- [ ] Greg matches the approved model when one exists.
- [ ] His scene adds a consequence or a payoff.
- [ ] The closing prompt follows the main payoff.
- [ ] Selected assets and sources are recorded.

## After export

- [ ] Play the actual file from beginning to end.
- [ ] Check on a phone and inspect platform-overlay placement.
- [ ] Check headphones and a phone speaker.
- [ ] Confirm resolution, frame rate, duration, and audio presence.
- [ ] Check the first and last frames, missing media, black flashes, and text substitution.
- [ ] Record the delivered filename and version.
- [ ] Record any deliberate exceptions or open choices.

## After upload

- [ ] Record platform, date, title, thumbnail or opening frame, and link.
- [ ] Record metrics at consistent observation windows.
- [ ] Describe retention changes with timestamps when available.
- [ ] Separate measured results from possible explanations.
- [ ] Choose a single clear next experiment.
