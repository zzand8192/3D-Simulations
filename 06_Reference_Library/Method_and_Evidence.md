## Inspection performed

All 25 files were decoded at native 30 fps, totaling 48,453 frames. Consecutive frames were compared computationally during the earlier analysis. Visual review used one frame every two seconds plus the final frame, alongside selected enlarged details. The resulting contact sheets are included. This was a sampled visual review, with computational decoding of all frames. No exhaustive human visual inspection of every frame is claimed.

Times in the notes are approximate scene examples from the actual file start. They are not an exhaustive verified edit list. Frame sheets show their sampled times explicitly. Use the video player for exact action and audio timing.

## Audio

The audio tracks were extracted and analyzed for energy and automatic speech recognition. Direct listening was unavailable. Automated recognition can miss speech and hallucinate words. The included machine transcript file is marked provisional. Music, instrument identity, exact individual effects, dialogue delivery, and full sound design remain unverified.

One measured example is Download (24).mp4, where mean audio energy rises roughly 17 dB between the preceding one-second interval and the sound arrival around 0:18. This supports a local timing contrast. It does not establish perceived loudness on a phone or a target mix level.
