## How to read the authority labels

- Confirmed direction means Isaac explicitly said it in an available message.
- Working default means an earlier assistant proposed it or this handbook recommends it. Isaac can revise it.
- Observed means it is visible in the reviewed reference footage or supported by file measurements.
- Interpretation means a proposed explanation of why a technique may hold attention.
- Open means a production choice or asset still needs to be created.

## Scope of the reference library

The library contains the original eight examples and the 17 additional examples Isaac supplied. These are 25 distinct supplied references. Download (8).mp4 was outside the supplied analysis set, so it has no assigned reference ID here.

Reference titles in this handbook are descriptive labels. View counts of 80,000+ with some exceeding one million were reported by Isaac for the additional examples. Individual counts, links, and retention analytics were unavailable. No specific technique is proven to have caused those results.

All 25 videos were decoded at 30 fps. Visual inspection used timestamped sampled sequences and selected enlarged details. The handbook does not claim a person manually examined every frame. Audio findings use levels, timing, and automated speech recognition. Specific music and many individual effects remain unverified.
