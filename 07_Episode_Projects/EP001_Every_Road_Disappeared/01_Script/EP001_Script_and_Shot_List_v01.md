## Greg continuity

- **Location:** MESA STOP gas station, Arizona desert. The old road ran along the front of the station.
- **Ordinary task:** buying lunch after filling up.
- **Prop and holding hand:** sandwich in his right hand and a red cup in his left (S11–S14). Car keys in his right hand at Month 6, put into his pocket at 85.6 s.
- **Event consequence:** there's no road to drive on.
- **Recognition and response:** a car–road–car head turn, a pause, then "I just filled up."
- **Return or final state:** the new road exists but is closed for final inspection, and Greg is still stuck.
