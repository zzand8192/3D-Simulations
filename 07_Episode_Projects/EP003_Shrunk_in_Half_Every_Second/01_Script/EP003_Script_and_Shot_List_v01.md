# EP003 script and shot list, v01

One continuous shot. No narrator, no voices, and Greg never speaks.

The camera follows Greg's shrink with a short lag: each halving reads as Greg dropping away, then the camera closing in again.

**Formula:** height = 1.8 m ÷ 2^n. Each halving is a 0.45 s eased pulse centred on the second it belongs to. The counter flips at video time 4.0 + n.

The jump keeps the shrink rate unchanged. Showing every second to the Planck length would add about 57 s of near-empty abstract footage.
