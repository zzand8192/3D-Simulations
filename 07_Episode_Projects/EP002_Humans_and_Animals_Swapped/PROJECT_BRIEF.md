# EP002 — What If Humans and Animals Swapped Places?

Stage: v01 rendering and awaiting Isaac's review · Runtime: 93 s · 1080 × 1920 at 30 fps · Owner: Isaac (direction), Claude (production)

## Premise

At one moment every human and animal on Earth swaps roles. Animals become the dominant species: they run cities, homes, businesses, transport and governments. Humans take the roles of pets, zoo animals and wildlife.

The swap is instant, worldwide and permanent. Animals stand and speak; humans keep human bodies but are treated like pets. There are no numeric claims.

## Audience promise

- Opening: an ordinary morning walk. The dog takes the leash: "Alright. My turn to walk you."
- Consequences:
  1. Society flips: a police dog, a cat CEO and a lion leader.
  2. Worldwide news (WORLD 24, Tokyo, London).
  3. Humans learn pet life: dog park, pet store, vet.
  4. Cities are redesigned for every species: cat towers, monkey rope bridges, tall arches.
- Ending: Year 3. Tom begs to go outside; Biscuit reluctantly takes him for a walk at sunset.

## Greg

- **Where:** a zoo in Sydney, Australia, sitting on a bench reading a newspaper.
- **What happens:** a kangaroo keeper tells him he's now one of the exhibits. The camera reveals the fence, the HUMANS sign and animal visitors with cameras.
- **His line:** "I was literally just visiting."
- **Model:** GREG_v1.

## Cast

Tom (human, blue tee with orange trim; collar after the swap), Biscuit (golden dog), police shepherd, cat CEO, Kevin (employee), lion leader, fox anchor, kangaroo keeper, park dogs, cat shopper and kitten, ginger-cat vet, and a mixed animal crowd in every city.
