# Studio operations

## Manage the creative work

Use a small production slate with stages of idea, researched, outline, animatic, assets, animation, edit, review, scheduled, published, and evaluated. Select the next stage based on what is actually complete. Record the owner if additional people join the studio. No team or schedule is assumed in this pack.

## Review gates

At concept review, confirm the premise, audience promise, and ending. At animatic review, confirm timing, clarity, UI, and Greg integration. At export review, check the delivered file. After publication, record actual results and one next experiment.

## Track effort and costs

For each episode, record planned and actual work hours, purchased assets, rendering or service costs, and reusable assets created. Use actual values. Keep a distinction between one-time reusable setup and episode-specific effort.

## Maintain the knowledge base

Update confirmed creative decisions in the decision log. Then revise the relevant guide, MASTER_BRIEF.md, and STUDIO_CONTEXT.json. Preserve the source and date of a change. Archive old versions when they are needed to understand a project.
