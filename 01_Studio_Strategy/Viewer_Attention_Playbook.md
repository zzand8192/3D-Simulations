# Viewer attention playbook

The mechanisms below describe observed choices and practical production suggestions. Their effects on retention are interpretations. Validate them against the studio's own upload data.

## Opening and premise

| Pattern | Evidence | Why it may sustain attention | Implementation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One altered rule | N16 wood, N19 light, N20 calcium | The audience understands the experiment quickly | Write one sentence defining the change and its limits |
| Familiar subject | N14 penny, N18 cat, N12 sneeze | Little setup is needed | Use an object or action viewers already know |
| Specific duration or multiplier | Five-second disappearance films and N18's 100x cat | Creates a clear boundary | State duration, scale, population, or distance |
| Question over an active scene | All 25 | Image and premise are available together | Put the opening question on the first useful shot |
| Early visual promise | N23 opens with a huge explosion before restarting | Shows what the episode will deliver | Show a brief glimpse or a clear starting condition |
| Personal camera position | N18 window, N23 explosion outside a window | Gives the danger a destination | Establish the viewer's physical location |
| Recognizable place | Piers, city streets, stadium, dam | Makes change easy to compare | Establish a few landmarks before the event |

## Narrative progression

| Pattern | Evidence | Why it may sustain attention | Implementation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Obvious consequence first | N16 missing deck | Establishes the rule | Demonstrate one direct effect |
| Less obvious consequence second | N13 food, cotton, money | Adds discovery | Research dependencies and illustrate several |
| Scale grows through milestones | N14 coffee to buildings to space | Gives a ladder of reveals | Plan meaningful thresholds |
| Several local payoffs | N17 chain of escalating mishaps | Rewards continued watching | Resolve small questions throughout |
| Temporary event, persistent damage | N20 restored calcium after dam failure | Changes the audience's expectation | Show aftermath after restoration |
| New threat after initial answer | N12 sneeze then ocean withdrawal | Opens a second question | Use a causally supported consequence |
| Reassurance followed by complication | Repeated restoration sequences | Briefly changes emotional direction | Allow relief before revealing remaining damage |
| Delayed information arrival | N24 sound travels after eruption | Turns waiting into the story | Separate visual onset and consequence when justified |
| Final callback | O01 ring, O05 ant | Connects early and late details | Plant the final object early |
| Next implication | N11 water must come back out | Leaves a specific follow-up question | Finish with one natural extension |

## Visual attention and camera

| Pattern | Evidence | Why it may sustain attention | Implementation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stable landmark for comparison | Ferris wheel and pier across the batch | Shows magnitude without explanation | Keep a known object in view |
| Approaching subject | N18 cat | Makes distance matter | Animate a clear path toward the camera |
| Frame becomes overwhelmed | Water walls and explosion clouds | Changes the image substantially | Let scale exceed the composition |
| Foreground prop | Phones, food, hands, straws | Grounds the viewpoint | Use a prop that supports the event |
| Wide-to-close variation | N13 stadium to hot dog at 0:18 | Reveals a new kind of evidence | Cut close for a small material consequence |
| Return to prior composition | N16 restored pier | Makes aftermath legible | Reuse a shot for comparison |
| Bright silhouettes | Shirt colors in crowds | Helps separate actions | Keep important figures readable |
| Light changes with stakes | N17 sunset after daytime mishaps, N24 ash darkens sky | Signals time or mood | Motivate lighting changes in the story |

## People and humor

| Pattern | Evidence | Why it may sustain attention | Implementation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary activity interrupted | Food, phone, spectators | Makes global change personal | Give characters an existing task |
| Crowd notices late | Beach observers linger before waves | Audience anticipates what characters will discover | Stage notice, investigation, reaction |
| Synchronized group behavior | N12 sneeze, N15 clap | Creates a large readable event | Animate a coherent reaction wave |
| One small misfortune | N10 hot dog | Adds human specificity | Reserve a prop or minor goal for a joke |
| Deadpan caption | Calm explanations beside huge events | Lets the image deliver the absurdity | Keep wording short and concrete |
| Recurring series beat | Tsunami in 13 of 25 | Gives returning viewers recognition | Use Greg as the studio's anticipated character beat |
| Character remains recognizable | Gold-clad figure in O05, O06, O07 | Encourages recognition across premises | Reuse Greg's final model |
| Silence or a look can carry a reaction | Suitable to the deadpan visual style | Avoids slowing every moment for dialogue | Animate a short glance, pause, or continued task |

## Editing, sound, and endings

| Pattern | Evidence | Why it may sustain attention | Implementation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Progress within a held shot | N18 and N24 | Anticipation survives without frequent cuts | Make the subject, counter, or environment change |
| Cut for new information | Hand-held details and broad scale shots | Every edit contributes evidence | Name the new fact each shot provides |
| Mark altered time | N13 shown 5x slower | Helps viewers follow durations | Label slow motion and time skips |
| Skip inactive intervals | Minutes-later cards and fast-forward cues | Moves to the next consequence | Omit uneventful waiting |
| Stretch arrival | Wave final-second sequences | Gives a defined endpoint time to resolve | Reserve slow motion for a meaningful instant |
| Audio contrast at a visual event | N24 measured level rise at 0:18 | Marks the arrival physically | Plan a quiet lead-in and synchronized arrival |
| Sound perspective changes | Proposed underwater and distance treatment | Reinforces viewpoint | Record as an implementation recommendation |
| Threat reaches the camera | Water, cat, cracked-screen endings | Completes the approach | Design an exact contact or aftermath frame |
| Premise returns on a dark card | All 25 | Reinforces the episode's identity | Briefly repeat the question after the payoff |
| Specific next action | N14 choice and N11 sequel prompt | Gives a concrete response | Ask one relevant question after the story |
